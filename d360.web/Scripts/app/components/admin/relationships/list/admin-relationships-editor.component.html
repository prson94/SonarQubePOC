Relationships

When creating a relationship type, Subject should always be the higher-level item in the relationship, while Object is the lower-level item in the relationship. For example, when defining a relationship between Application and Business Term you would set Application as Subject and Business Term as Object. This will impact how sourcing and synonym inheritance works, as the Object is what you are sourcing as well as where synonyms defined on the relationship will also appear.
