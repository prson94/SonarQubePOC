Weight(%)
Weight determines the contributions of this measure to the overall score calculated for an asset. For example, a measure with a weight of 50% will contribute twice as much as a measure with a weight of 25%. The sum of all measures used to calculate the score should sum to 100% (at the top-level with and within each group of measures. Where they do not, they will be adjusted when used to calculate the score.
