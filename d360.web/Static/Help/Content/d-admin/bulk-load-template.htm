Bulk loading

In other help topics, the methods that are described for adding new items, such as adding new Business Terms or Applications, are typically used to make small numbers of entries. To add large volumes of information, you should use bulk loading.

The bulk loader allows you to make multiple entries onto a spreadsheet which can then be uploaded into Infogix Data3Sixty in one go. For example, you can use bulk loading to upload a series of Business Term definitions.

Note: As a first step, you must first ensure that any custom fields required for the artifact are already in place, see Defining fields on asset types.

 

Bulk loading business terms

The following example illustrates the process of bulk loading business terms to Infogix Data3Sixty:

 

  1. Click the Administration icon on the left of the screen and select Bulk Loader.
  2. In the top right corner of the History panel, click the Add button:

  3. In the Upload a spreadsheet job panel, leave the default Action setting of Promotion, as the Business Terms will be uploaded and promoted to the glossary.
  4. Select the Relationships Type of Artifact: Business Term. This is the type of artifact that will be uploaded. You will see a depiction of the spreadsheet columns, with any mandatory columns shown in bold.
  5. Click the Download Spreadsheet link.
  6. In each column of the spreadsheet, make the required entries. Any custom fields that you have created will be included in your spreadsheet.
  7. Return to the Infogix Data3Sixty user interfact and click Choose file to navigate to your spreadsheet.
  8. Click Save to bulk upload the business terms.
