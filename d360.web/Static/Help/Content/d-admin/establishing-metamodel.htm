Establishing the metamodel

Infogix Data3Sixty Govern is a highly configurable application, providing a great deal of flexibility for customizing the system to provide a solution to match the specific data governance requirements of any organization.

The Metamodel refers to the configurable information architecture that system administrators build in order to support the tasks required of the Data Stewards, Business Owners and Data Protection Officers who will be working with the system to support their data governance initiatives. If you have not already read the User guide, it is recommended that you read this guide to understand how the system will be used day to day by these typical business users.

 

Before building out the Metamodel of your installation you should already have a good idea about how you wish to structure your governance efforts, including:

  • Artifact types: eg. Business Terms, Applications, Processes
  • Top-level models:Investments, Retail Banking, Human Resources, Sales
  • Quality rules and API integrations
  • Top-level policies: GDPR, CECL, Dodd Frank
  • Relationships: eg. policy governs business term, model groups business term, quality rule validates business term
