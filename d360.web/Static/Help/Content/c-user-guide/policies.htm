Policies

One of the primary reasons for any organization to undertake a data governance effort is to ensure their own compliance with governmental statutes and regulations. These statutes and regulations can be cataloged in Infogix Data3Sixty and then associated with business terms and other artifacts.

Why create policy assets?

You should create Policy assets in Infogix Data3Sixty to ensure that everyone in your organization has one common definition for a particular policy, and thereby has a common understanding of what compliance with that policy entails. Policies, like models, can be depicted graphically, so that you can quickly and easily visualize the policy and every other type of artifact with which it interfaces.

Listed below are some well known regulations that Infogix Data3Sixty users commonly record as artifacts. Your organization will create those policies which are relevant to your own business:

Data Protection Regulations

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): A regulation adapted by European Parliament, the Council of the European Union, and the European Commission that strengthens and unifies personal data protection for all individuals within the European Union (EU), and limits the export of personal data outside the EU.

Banking Regulations

Basel III: A global, voluntary regulatory framework intended to strengthen bank capital requirements by increasing bank liquidity and decreasing bank leverage.

MiFID: A regulation that increases transparency across the European Union's financial markets and standardizes the regulatory disclosures required for particular markets.
