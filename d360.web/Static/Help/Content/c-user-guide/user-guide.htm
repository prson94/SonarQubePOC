User guide

Infogix Data3Sixty Govern is a cloud-based, data governance platform software tool that allows you to effectively define, track, and manage your data assets.

Data governance describes a set of repeatable, scalable strategies and technologies that ensure that important data assets stay in compliance with corporate policies and government regulations. As data volumes grow much faster than any other type of resource, data can quickly become outdated and difficult to access. Data can lack quality or be incomplete, leading to:

  • Inaccurate reporting
  • Bad decision-making
  • Time spent on remediation
  • Lack of effective business practices
  • Non-compliance with governmental regulations

In many businesses, data management has traditionally been a function of Information Technology (IT) departments. While IT departments have substantial technical expertise, they sometimes lack knowledge of the business context of the data. Business context knowledge is required to make critical decisions. One goal of data governance is to put the control and responsibility for data into the purview of the core business.

Every asset catalogued in Infogix Data3Sixty Govern is assigned to a responsible party such as a business owner or data steward. Its relationship to other assets is thoroughly documented. This gives the user a thorough understanding of the data and any and all relationships that exist between the data and the other business assets that make use of that data, such as reports, applications, or processes.

This user guide will take you through all of the activities you will need to perform in your day to day duties as:

  • Business owners
  • Data stewards
  • Team supervisors
  • Chief Protection Officers

 

Infogix Data3Sixty Govern is a highly customizable application, configured to support the specific requirements of your own organizations governance strategy. For those users who are required to perform administration tasks, such as establishing and maintaining the framework and integration of your own individual implementation of the system, a separate administration guide is also provided. This guide covers activities more common to:

  • Team supervisors
  • Technical custodians
  • System administrators
