Models

Models are used to organize artifacts into hierarchical structures, typically broken down by business function, such as Human Resources and Finances. You might choose to establish a separate model for each function, possible broken down further into sub-domains. The Human Resources function might have sub-groupings for Payroll, Benefits, and Recruitment.

Each model can be assigned its own properties (business metadata) and responsible party (business owner, data steward). Related artifacts can then inherit responsibility assignments from associated models.
