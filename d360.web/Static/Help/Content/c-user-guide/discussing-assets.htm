Discussing assets

When you are viewing an item in Infogix Data3Sixty, for example a Business Term or a Report, you can view a community summary for the artifact. The summary shows the overall data governance score for the artifact, any comments or actions and the item workflow status:

You can view existing comments by clicking Comments in the summary panel. To add a new comment:

  1. Click Comments.
  2. Type your comment in the Comments panel, then click Post.
