Developing artifacts for certification

Having created a draft artifact, you should then concentrate on developing it further to:

  • ensure that the correct users or groups have responsibility for the item
  • capture relationships to models, grouping your artifact into taxonomies and hierarchies
  • capture relationships to quality rules and policies, to govern your artifact
  • capture relationships to other artifacts, such as business terms, applications, processes and reports

You can then request for your artifact to be certified as a fully defined, quality asset in the Glossary.
